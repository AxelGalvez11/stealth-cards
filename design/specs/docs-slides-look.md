# Lucida's look for Note and Presentation pages (the "Docs and Slides" pass)

The owner, 2026-10-12: "I like the way the docs, the presentation, and the artifacts look… could you recreate that for Lucida."
Studied from the owner's own Claude Docs and Slides artifacts (Lucida Philosophy Scripts, Final Project Outline, Faithmaxxing,
Faithmaxxing Teacher Notes) in his browser. What follows is what makes them feel good, written as Lucida's own design: no
code, fonts or marks are taken from Anthropic's apps.

## What makes the Docs feel good

| Trait | What it is |
|---|---|
| Quiet chrome | One thin top bar: home, the title with ▾ (rename, move), and on the right only who's here, comments, ask AI, Share. A second slim row of insert tools (table, image, checklist, + more). No sidebar of the app around it. |
| A reading column | About 660 px wide, starting a third of the way in, on plain white; nothing boxed, no borders. |
| Two typefaces | Titles and headings in a refined serif, regular weight, tight; body in a calm sans at about 17 px, line height about 1.45, near-black (not pure black). |
| Clear, small hierarchy | Title ~40 px serif · section ~26 px serif · sub-section ~20 px serif · body 17 px sans. Bold run-in labels ("Date:", "Hook:") instead of extra headings. |
| Tabs inside a page | A left rail of the page's tabs (sub-pages) with + New tab; the open one is a soft gray pill. |
| Links as chips | A link to another page is a small gray rounded chip with its title. |
| Space | Generous top margin, ~10 px between paragraphs, ~36 px above a section heading. |

## What makes the Slides feel good

| Trait | What it is |
|---|---|
| A stage, not a page | The slide sits centered on a warm light-gray canvas with a soft shadow; zoom shown as a %. |
| Floating tool pills | Top left: insert (text, image, table, shape ▾). Top right: download, present ▶, notes panel, zoom. White, rounded, small. |
| Filmstrip | Numbered thumbnails along the bottom; + to add; a toggle to a **grid of all slides grouped by section**, each section headed by one sentence of what it does. |
| Slide type | Huge tight grotesk headlines (bold, slightly negative tracking); small UPPERCASE letter-spaced eyebrow labels above them; italic serif for quotes and taglines; hairline rules over columns; big numerals (1 2 3) for steps. |
| Rhythm | Dark and light slides alternate for emphasis; one idea per slide; heading always at the top margin. |
| A fixed canvas | 1920×1080, a small safe set of elements (headings, text, lists, tables, images, shapes, icons, connectors), so an AI writes them reliably. |
| Notes for the presenter | Speaker notes per slide; a teacher-notes view with Back / Next that follows the deck. |

## Lucida's version

**Note pages (now):**
- The page opens full width with Lucida's slim top bar (back to the deck as a chip, the title ▾, version, ⋯) and a slim insert row (callout, chain, table, image, card, + more), not the app's sidebar.
- A reading column of ~660 px. Title and headings in a serif (proposed: **Newsreader** from Google Fonts, regular, tight), body in Lucida's Geist at 17 px / 1.5, near-black.
- Tabs inside a page: a left rail of the page's pages inside it, + New page inside.
- Page links and card links as small gray chips inline; embedded maps as today's preview boxes.
- Callouts, chains, tables and toggles restyled to the quieter document look (hairlines, no heavy fills; the trap / pearl / mnemonic colors kept as a thin left bar and label).

**Presentation pages (step 5):**
- The editor as a stage: warm gray canvas, centered 16:9 slide with soft shadow, floating insert / present / notes / zoom pills, a numbered filmstrip, and a sections grid.
- The slide format: 1920×1080, a closed set of elements an AI writes reliably; Lucida's own slide themes using Geist for headlines, the serif for quotes, uppercase eyebrows, hairline rules, big numerals, dark/light rhythm.
- Present mode, PDF export, speaker notes and a presenter view with Back / Next.
- Study twist: any slide → "+ Card"; a deck of slides can be made from a Note or a mind map by Lucida's AI or yours.
